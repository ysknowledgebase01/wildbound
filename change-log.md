# Wildbound change log

What changed on Wildbound: the server's add-on (the mod), the server itself, and
this website. Newest first. Version numbers are the add-on's (v1.451 = 1.451.0).

Each day has up to three parts:

- **Add-on**: changes to the game on the server.
- **Server**: changes to how the server runs or how you join it.
- **Website**: changes to the Mob Index and the welcome pages.

---

## 2026-11-07

### Add-on (v1.491)

- **The newest mobs are whole.** Left legs, ears, arms and wings were missing from Vampire
  Villager, Count Thirst, Dark Stallion, Ender Centaur, Lord of Divinity, Elder Overlord, Hopfu,
  Shadow Hopper, Noman and Leviathan. They're back.
- **Kagerog shows up again.** It was invisible.
- **Count Thirst's bats** circle and flap around him.
- **Real walking.** Ender Centaur, Lord of Divinity and the other new mobs step at their real
  speed. Toadhan, Kagerog, Hopfu and Shadow Hopper walk on two legs instead of sliding.
- **Leviathan swims** around its water instead of lying still.
- **Toadhan and Kagerog can swim** like other frogs, and both now have a frog egg.
- **Lord of Divinity is bigger.**
- **Night Daggerotts grow stronger far from spawn,** like other wild mobs.

### Add-on (v1.490)

- **Lions and Toadhan grow stronger far from spawn.** Wild Lion Cubs, Wild Lions and Toadhan
  now level up with distance like every other wild mob, so far out they match the mobs around them.
- **Higher levels are harder to catch.** The higher a mob's level, the lower its catch chance.
  A Lv.40 mob is not easy to catch: a Lv.40 sheep is under 10% at full health. Tower mobs,
  tower captains and bosses get harder with level too, on top of their own catch rules.
- **More frogs.** Any still water attracts frogs if you wait, even a single block or a big lake.
  On rainy days frogs come much sooner, and they also hop about on open ground.
  Plain frogs can now come onto claimed land too.
- **Rabbits and Hopfu in more places.** Small groups of rabbits now turn up on open grass, dirt,
  sand and snow away from claimed land, and sometimes one of them is a Hopfu.
- **Town Guards level up.** A town's Guards are stronger the farther the town is from spawn,
  a bit above the wild mobs around it. Their level shows in their name.

### Add-on (v1.489)

- **The ten newest mobs show up again.** Vampire Villager, Count Thirst, Dark Stallion,
  Ender Centaur, Lord of Divinity, Elder Overlord, Hopfu, Shadow Hopper, Noman and
  Leviathan were invisible or only half drawn. Now their whole body shows.
- **Count Thirst's bats** now fly around him properly instead of looking frozen.
- **New eggs.** Each of those ten mobs has its own spawn egg and ball picture now, instead
  of a plain piglin or horse egg. Kagerog's egg is now gold, so it doesn't look like Toadhan's.

### Add-on (v1.488)

- **Storage Guard.** The Storekeeper at every Storage and Storage Locker is now the Storage
  Guard. Talk to it to sort your storage by type, by name, rarest first or most first.
  It sorts Box 1, 2 and 3 as one: part stacks of the same thing are joined up first, then
  everything is laid out again from Box 1. Enchantments, names and box contents are kept.
- **Sort my mobs.** Crouch and tap a Mob Chest, then pick Sort my mobs to lay out all your
  stored mobs again: highest level, rarest first, by kind, by name, or just closing the gaps.
- **Admin buildings** can now be built on claimed land.

### Add-on (v1.487)

- **Storage Locker.** A small 3x3 storage building with three barrels and a Storage Guard in
  the middle. Build it on your own land with the Building Deed, one on each piece of land.
  The barrels are your Box 1, 2 and 3, the same as at every Storage, and only you can see
  what's in yours. Hold the Building Deed and tap the locker to take it down. Your things
  stay in your storage.
- **Storage buildings** now put an open box away before they're removed, so nothing in it
  is lost.

## 2026-11-06

### Add-on (v1.486)

- **God class.** The six strongest Mythics are now God class: Ender Overlord, Elder Overlord,
  Lord of Divinity, Valley of Doom, Eye of the End and Leviathan. Hits from your own God
  ignore 25 percent of defence.
- **The strongest mobs were rebalanced.** Ender Overlord is now the strongest mob, and Ender
  King and Grand Llama are the top Legends. Valley of Doom and Eye of the End keep low life
  but hit hardest. Leviathan keeps its high life, but hits softer once it's yours.
- **Stat changes.** Ender Overlord, Lord of Divinity, Valley of Doom, Eye of the End, Ender
  King, Grand Llama, Elder Dragon and Arch Warden got stronger or changed shape. Leviathan,
  Ender Centaur, Noman, Dark Stallion, Count Thirst, Sentinel, Kagerog and Shadow Hopper
  got weaker, and that includes the ones you already own.
- **Console Block menu fixed.** It wouldn't open for land owners and co-owners. It works
  again on every land.

### Add-on (v1.485)

- **Console Blocks can be removed by mining.** A land's owners can mine theirs, and admins
  can remove any of them. If a land's Console Block has gone missing, Utility gives
  you a new one.
- **Withers near claimed land** no longer break blocks. While one is close to claimed land,
  other mobs can't change blocks or pick up items either.
- **Sculk stays off claimed land.** When a mob dies on or near claimed land, any sculk
  catalyst close by pops out as an item.

### Add-on (v1.484)

- **Mining mobs look busy.** A working mob leans into the wall, with digging sounds and
  stone chips flying off.
- **Mining Outposts look like mines.** They now have a stone and gravel floor, a rail
  track with a minecart, ore in the walls and lantern posts. Existing outposts update by
  themselves.

### Add-on (v1.483)

- **Mining Outposts use a plain barrel.** Open it like any barrel and put one caught mob's
  ball in to start digging. Take the ball out to stop. Finds land in the barrel, and a
  second ball comes straight back to you.
- **Working mobs now show up** at the outpost.
- **Removing an outpost** drops what's in its barrel on the ground, and its mob goes to
  your Mob Chest.

### Add-on (v1.482)

- **Building Deed now works like the Shop Deed.** It has the same look and the same steps:
  place it, pick a building, check the outline, tap the deed, choose which way the front
  faces, and build.
- **Building needs less empty space.** Only the blocks the building uses have to be free,
  and plants are replaced. If something is in the way, the message says what and where.

### Add-on (v1.481)

- **Building Deed fixed.** It's now a block you can see. Place it on your own land, pick a
  building, check the glowing outline, then tap the deed to build. The deed comes back
  to you afterwards.
- **Take buildings down.** Hold your deed and tap an outpost or fountain to remove it.
  Fountains built before this update are removed by hand.
- **Trait Crystals** now show their icon.
- **Mob Menu.** Crouch next to your mob and tap Mob Menu for Traits, Stats & perks and
  more. Forge and Release stay at the Mob Chest.
- **Empty trait slots** now show on the Traits button and in the Mob Chest.

### Add-on (v1.480)

- **Fixed the ten new mobs.** A settings mistake stopped their attacks and animations
  from working.

### Add-on (v1.479)

- **Ten new mobs.** Lord of Divinity, Ender Centaur, Elder Overlord and Leviathan
  (Mythic), Dark Stallion, Shadow Hopper, Count Thirst and Noman (Legend), and Hopfu and
  Vampire Villager (Elite). Each has its own attacks and traits, and the Mob Index lists
  their mixes.
- **Hopfu in the wild.** Some wild rabbits are Hopfu, Lv.12 or higher. They leave you alone
  until you hit them or throw a ball.
- **Snow Golems in the wild.** Some Strays in snowy places come out as Snow Golems instead.
- **Leviathan.** Beat 31 sea creatures in the ocean within 30 minutes, then stay over deep
  water for a small chance that a Lv.70 Leviathan rises. It is very hard to catch.

### Add-on (v1.478)

- **Every mob has two trait slots.** Mobs keep their own four traits, and two more
  slots are filled from a pool of traits from their parents, crystals and Lv.30 and
  Lv.40. Swap them on the mob's card with the new Traits button. Existing mobs start
  with both slots empty, so set yours after the update.
- **Better traits for many mobs.** End mobs, wind mobs, sea hunters, armored mobs and
  young dragons have new traits that suit them, and the five titans now have four
  traits each. Gills is gone: aquatic mobs get Tough instead.
- **Every Legend and Mythic has its own signature trait,** with 14 new ones. Wild ones
  use them too, so some boss fights are harder.
- **Second awakening moved to +30.** The + now goes up to +30. Mobs already at +10
  keep their second awakening.
- **Mixing averages the +.** A mix takes its parents' average +, then adds 1 to 3, up
  to +10. Past that only Raid Bars add more. Each + also gives a perk point.
- **Raid Bosses are Lv.45 to 65,** whatever mob you bring. Monolith Raid Shards go to
  players who landed a hit.
- **Ender Cores from the Quest Master.** Land a hit on 15 Monolith bosses to earn one.
- **New Quest Master.** Item requests are replaced by quests of your own: hunts, tower
  clears and boss fights. Friends nearby share kills, and each player gets a free swap
  every 6 hours.
- **The Sanctuary.** At the Mob Trainer, pay 30 coins to send a mob away for good and
  keep one of its traits in a crystal. Crystals can be traded.
- **Building Deed.** Buy one for 50 coins at the Furniture Shop to build on your own
  land. It's yours to keep and reuse.
- **Mining Outposts.** Put a mob to work on your land. It digs up mostly dirt, plus some
  ores and shards, while you're online, wherever you are.
- **Water Fountains** for your own land, just for looks.
- **Dirt is scarcer.** It's gone from Utility, and the Builder's Depot sells 12 for 1 coin.

### Website

- Updated the Quest Master, Mob Trainer, Furniture Shop, Builder's Depot, Raid Shard,
  Raid Bar, Awakening Crystal and Ender Core pages for v1.478.

### Add-on (v1.477)

- **Mobs far from spawn are just higher level.** Levelled mobs no longer show a star
  or "Elite" in their names: they read like "Zombie Lv.6". Levelled wild mobs and the
  rare random ones are no harder to catch than any other.
- **Element and race mixing.** Lower mobs now mix as their element and their race.
  Fire mobs count as a Magma Cube, Poison mobs as a Spider, Frost mobs as a Stray,
  Undead mobs as a Zombie, Void mobs as an Enderman and Storm mobs as a Breeze. A
  Venom Sheep can stand in for a Spider or a Sheep, for example. Exact recipes always
  come first, levels still apply, and two special mobs never turn into a plain mob
  this way. Every special mob now has something it can mix into.
- **Mob Towers and Bases have floors.** Ground floor guards are the lighter special
  mobs, the second floor holds stronger, useful ones, and Captains are strong elites or
  lower bosses. Places already standing change the next time they're retaken.
- **Captains are much harder to catch.** Every Captain is as hard to catch as a Raid
  Boss at any level, and can't be caught for 30 seconds after 10 balls. Guards are
  harder to catch too, and the second floor harder still.
- **Wither Skeleton is now an elite,** with stronger stats, and appears on the second
  floor of Grave places. Wild ones are tougher too.
- **Fixes:** Venom Sheep and Bone Sheep have their faces and skin back, Hex Cats walk
  properly, and a mob that was caught next to another one no longer brings a second
  copy out of its ball.

### Add-on (v1.476)

- **New Gold Golem recipes.** Mix a Golden Pig and an Iron Golem, or two Iron Golems
  that are both Lv.12 or higher. The old Piglin + Iron Golem, Copper Golem + Piglin
  and Zombified Piglin + Iron Golem mixes no longer make one.

### Add-on (v1.475)

- **Paired legendary attacks:** Dark Knight fires dark skulls, Dragon Knight
  fires blue wind beams, Ender Golem launches slow homing void orbs, Kraken
  sends bubble streams and Kagerog launches flying shadow swords while keeping
  its leap and close sword strike. Dark Mage gains a close energy field that
  pushes enemies away without harming itself.
- **Different volley styles:** Kraken, Kagerog and Dragon Knight fire 2–4
  smaller shots that can each damage the same enemy. Dark Mage, Grand Llama,
  Ender King and Arch Warden aim at up to 2–4 different enemies, with damage
  from each shot. Explosive volleys damage each enemy once, including splash
  and split shots; Sentinel keeps its existing missile rule.
- **Ender Overlord:** void orbs split into shadow swords. Its claw, close void
  blast and teleport gap closer give it a different attack pattern from Ender King.
- Attacks preserve blocks and respect caught-mob ownership and battle targets.

## 2026-11-05

### Add-on (v1.474)

- **Rainy-day encounters:** eligible wild cows, sheep, pigs, chickens, mooshrooms
  and rabbits have a 30% Frog / 30% Toadhan / 40% unchanged roll during rain.
  Nearby existing animals are checked too, once per rainy spell. No pond is needed;
  named, tamed, leashed and claimed animals are protected.
- **Neutral Toadhan:** wild Toadhan waits until attacked or a catch is attempted,
  then retaliates against its provoker. Caught Toadhan keeps its usual battle behavior.
- **Admin weather controls:** Admin Wand -> Weather offers Clear, Rain and
  Thunderstorm for 1-30 minutes. Rain detection also checks a storm already underway
  when the pack starts.

### Add-on (v1.473)

- **Toadhan:** a fast elite martial fighter. Mix Piglin with Frog, Toxic Frog or
  Ember Frog, both Lv.5. Find it as a tower captain or a rare Lv.15-20 replacement
  for wild zombies and spiders exposed to rain; no pond is needed.
- **Kagerog:** a legendary twin-sword fighter. Mix Toadhan and Dragon Squire,
  both Lv.30. It leaps toward its target and delivers a downward crossing strike.
  Both fighters use normal level growth, capture and battle rules.
- **Frog ponds:** more frogs appear near small pools during rain. Sneak-use a
  sponge on the bank of an existing pond with 4-64 connected water blocks to mark
  it without draining it. Frogs can arrive after one day cycle.
- **Ember Frog:** the existing fire frog appears near magma in the Nether and
  withstands fire and lava. Existing catches and recipes keep their identity.
- **Anglerfish:** night and low-light replacement chances are higher, and dark
  underground water can qualify. Its luminous parts use the same glow material
  as Glow Squid. Local population limits remain.

### Add-on (v1.471)

- **Players first.** Wild Anglerfish and Sentinel prioritize visible players,
  then retaliate against attackers. Uninvolved sea life no longer distracts them.
- **More ocean encounters.** The eligible ocean-floor replacement chance is now
  30%, up from 15%; existing population limits remain.
- **Sentinel missile salvos.** It fires 2-4 missiles with small course corrections.
  Evasive movement can make them miss. Overlapping missiles and blasts damage
  each enemy at most once per salvo, and leave blocks intact.
- **Missiles are not creatures.** Looking at a torpedo no longer shows mob stats.
- **Level shark bodies.** All shark kinds, including Megalodon, keep their bodies
  level while rising or descending. Their tail motion is preserved.

### Add-on (v1.470)

- **Ocean-floor spawning repaired.** Anglerfish and Sentinel can now find the
  seabed when replacing eligible natural ocean spawns. The original chances and
  local population limits stay the same.
- **Slow swimming restored.** Both keep cruising near the seabed while watching
  distant prey, instead of stopping whenever an enemy is in sight.
- **Curving shots.** Energy balls and torpedoes follow moving enemies with gradual
  turns. Torpedoes still leave the launcher straight before turning. Shots check
  actual body hits, including tall enemies, and stop at walls; blasts leave blocks intact.

### Add-on (v1.469)

- **Anglerfish:** a slow ocean-floor predator with a glowing lure, ranged energy
  shots and a close-range bite. Mix Shark + Shardion, both Lv.15 or higher.
- **Sentinel:** a legendary armored variant with high health and defense, slow
  movement and torpedoes that damage nearby enemies without breaking blocks.
  Mix Anglerfish + Chrome Golem, both Lv.30 or higher.
- Both use the existing level growth, inheritance and combat systems. Wild ones
  stay near the seabed and can climb submerged obstacles; caught ones float and
  follow their owner. Underwater torpedoes leave bubbles; in air they use exhaust.

### Website

- Updated Frog and Toadhan encounter information in the Mob Index.

- Added Toadhan and Kagerog recipes, portraits and stats to the Mob Index, plus
  updated frog pond, Ember Frog and Anglerfish encounter information.

- Added Anglerfish and Sentinel to the Mob Index, with their mix ingredients,
  minimum parent levels, portraits and links from the parent mobs.

## 2026-11-04

### Add-on (v1.468)

- **Lions attack on sight.** Lions and cubs now go after anything they see:
  players, caught mobs, villagers, traders, animals and hostile mobs. Tamed,
  named, leashed or ridden animals are still left alone.
- **Wild Lions are tougher.** They're now Lv.20-30, a little rarer and harder to
  catch.
- **Frost Elk are tougher.** They're now Lv.15-20 wherever they appear, including
  snowy areas, and harder to catch.
- **Snakes ignore other monsters.** Wild snakes no longer attack hostile mobs, and
  don't fight back against them. Caught snakes still fight for you.
- **A caught Wither that fights.** It flies, hunts enemies, comes to help you and
  has no boss bar. Its three heads fire at up to three different enemies at once,
  and it does this while you ride it too.
- **No more wither roses.** Roses left behind where a Wither kills something are
  now cleared away.

### Add-on (v1.467)

- **Lions on the prowl.** About 1 in 10 natural farm animals, like cows, pigs,
  horses and sheep, now appears as a lion instead. Most are Lion Cubs (Lv.5-10)
  and some are Wild Lions (Lv.15-20).
- **Lions hunt wild animals.** They go after animals like cows, pigs and horses.
  They leave players, caught mobs, villagers and any animal that's tamed, named,
  on a lead or being ridden alone. Like other hostile mobs, they're cleared from
  claimed land and the safe spawn area.
- **More Frost Elk.** One in five goats now appears as a Frost Elk.
- **Room for everyone.** Animals stop turning into lions once an area has two
  Wild Lions or two cubs, and goats stop turning into Frost Elk once one is
  nearby.
- **Snowy encounters sooner.** Frost goats, frost horses and Frost Elk can now
  show up the first time you visit a snowy area.

### Add-on (v1.466)

- **Wolf-built mobs rebuilt.** 23 mobs, including the Archfiend, Minotaur,
  Unixion, Naga, Soulreaper and the Wild Lion, were secretly wolves under the
  hood and kept their pet habits. They now use the same plain fighter brain as
  the Shadow Knight, to help caught ones go after enemies instead of waiting for
  them to come close. Their looks, stats and drops stay the same, and leads no
  longer work on them.
- **Wild ones stay focused.** These mobs no longer call for help, and no longer
  turn on players after chasing prey.
- **Skeletons are braver.** They now run only from real wolves, not from every
  mob that used to be built on one.
- **Lion Cubs can bite.** A caught Lion Cub chased enemies but could never hurt
  them with a bite.

### Add-on (v1.465)

- **Towers fight at the level they promise.** Tower and base guards now match
  the level on the notice, and guards are a few levels below their captain.
- **Wild mobs get tougher the further you travel.** Ordinary wild mobs go up 5
  levels for every 200 blocks from spawn, up to Lv.40. The level is set once and
  doesn't change as a mob wanders.
- **Monoliths come back.** Old expired Monoliths no longer block new ones, new
  ones appear closer to you, and your progress toward one survives a restart.

### Add-on (v1.464)

- **Caught mobs look for trouble.** They now search for hostile mobs near them
  instead of waiting to be attacked, and a direct order comes first.
- **More ways to give an order.** Using a caught ball on a mob, or aiming it at
  one you can see, sends your mob after it. Walls block an order.
- **Tower and base golems fight.** Golems guarding a tower now attack players
  and their mobs. Catch one and it stops. Ordinary golems are unchanged.

### Add-on (v1.463)

- **More land in the south.** The mainland now reaches further south, with new
  offshore habitats. Land that was already there stays as it was.
- **Tower levels follow distance.** They rise with distance from spawn and the
  level of nearby players. Your deployed mob's level doesn't count.
- **Tougher catches.** Boss-tier captains and bounty mobs are a little harder to
  catch.
- **Daggerotts in the Nether.** They can now appear in place of some Magma Cubes.
- **Frost mobs and powder snow.** Frost mobs now stay on top of powder snow.

## 2026-11-03

### Add-on (v1.462)

- **Calmer walking.** Big mobs no longer scurry on tiny fast steps. The
  Archfiend, Dreadlord, Drakari, Dragonlord, Minotaur, Brutaurus and Ender
  Overlord now stride like a zombie or the Shadow Knight, and bigger mobs
  take slower, longer steps. Snakes slither slowly, and the four-legged
  mobs (Grizzly, lions, Manticore, Kirin, Unixion, Frost Elk, Everfrost)
  walk at a natural pace. When a mob only creeps or gets pushed, its legs
  barely move.
- **Big mobs face what they hit.** A boss or Mythic no longer strikes an
  enemy standing behind it. It turns to face the enemy first.
- **Poison works again in battle.** Poison, Wither, burning and lava now
  hurt mobs as much as they should for their level. Before, they barely
  scratched a high-level mob. Poison still can't finish a mob off.
- **Fire from mobs goes out.** Fire started by a blaze's or ghast's
  fireball, or by lightning, now burns out after a few seconds.
- **Give, not Ride.** Holding a Raid Bar, Awakening Crystal, Ender Core,
  Glimmerwater, Revive Potion or Mob Tonic, the button on your own mob says
  **Give**, so you hand it over instead of climbing on.
- **Flying mounts.** Look down to fly lower and jump to fly higher. Get off
  in the air (or put it back in its ball) and you float down slowly.
- **Reaper Hunt**: the Reaper now looks like a Soulreaper. It can't be hit
  or pushed, but it can still catch you.
- **Balloon Pop**: while a round is on, only your hands pop balloons. Other
  items, Mob Balls included, can't be used.
- **Minigame fields**: when a round starts, wild animals and mobs are
  cleared from the field. Pets and named mobs are moved just outside. Any
  that wander in during the round are cleared the same way. Mobs from a game give no XP
  and drop nothing, and show no level or health labels.

## 2026-11-02

### Add-on (v1.454 to v1.461)

- New **living axes** (v1.461): two huge flying demon axes with uneven,
  spiked blades.
  - **Oathbreaker** (boss): Daggerott + Void Drake (both Lv.20). Also a
    boss in raids, on Bounty Board posters and in some Mob Towers.
  - **Valley of Doom** (Mythic): Minotaur + Oathbreaker (both Lv.40). Also
    in Ender Monolith raids and the Boss Portal.
  - When one spots an enemy, it leaps high into the air and dives straight
    down onto it, hitting everything around where it lands.
  - They are slow, but hit very hard. Every hit rolls a power die and can
    land up to twice as hard. With your own axe, you see the roll above
    your hotbar.
  - Their hits knock enemies far back, and nothing knocks them back.
  - While your Valley of Doom fights beside you, you get stronger too.
  - Ride your own one and you hold it in your hand, like the living swords.
    It drinks your health the same way. A short press makes you jump and
    slam down, hitting everything around where you land.
- **Mythic mixes** now follow one rule: a two-mob mix needs both mobs at
  Lv.40, and a four-of-a-kind mix needs Lv.30 (v1.461). Eye of the End,
  Endbringer, Dreadlord, Dragonlord (two Drakari) and Megalodon (two Great
  Whites) moved up from Lv.30 to Lv.40. Megalodon's four-Shark mix is gone.
- Abyscleaver's mix now needs both mobs at Lv.20, up from Lv.12 (v1.461).
- New **living swords** (v1.460): flying devil swords with a skull engraved
  on the blade and an eye in its forehead. They fly, so they chase flying
  mobs too.
  - **Daggerott**: Phantom + Magma Cube (both Lv.5). It also turns up at
    night now and then. On a **Blade Night** (told to everyone at dusk),
    small packs of them come out.
  - **Abyscleaver** (boss): Fire Dragon + Daggerott (both Lv.12). Also a
    boss in raids, on Bounty Board posters and in some Mob Towers.
  - **Eye of the End** (Mythic): Abyscleaver + Archfiend (both Lv.30). Also
    in Ender Monolith raids and the Boss Portal.
  - Daggerott and Abyscleaver charge in to stab. Abyscleaver and Eye of the
    End spin to cut everything around them. Eye of the End throws bursts of
    small daggers from far away.
  - Ride your own one and you hold it in your hand as a sword instead. It
    hits harder than a netherite sword and grows with your level and your
    mob's. A short press is its special move; hold to let it go. While you
    hold it, it drinks your health, and lets go before you drop too low. A
    mob with the Drain trait wins some of that back as you hit.
- The Archfiend, Dreadlord, Drakari, Dragonlord, Minotaur, Brutaurus and
  Ender Overlord walk with long, heavy steps instead of quick little ones
  (v1.460).
- New **Potion Store** (v1.458). It sells potions that heal your caught
  mobs, plus the Shop's potions for you:
  - **Mob Tonic** (5 coins): heals a hurt mob by half. It can't wake a
    fainted one (v1.459).
  - **Crystal Vial** (10 coins): hold it at a town Fountain to fill it with
    **Glimmerwater**, which wakes a fainted mob at half health. The vial is
    then empty again, and refilling is free. Vials don't stack.
  - **Revive Potion** (20 coins): full health, even for a fainted mob.
  - Hold one to use it, or hold it and tap your mob that's out.
- New **Mob Mixer** (v1.457): mix mobs with a friend, and you each get the
  new mob. Pick each other at the Mob Mixer, each put 1 or 2 mobs in your
  Mix Box, and one of you pays 30 to 50 coins to start it. Only real
  recipes work there, no forging.
- The health and level label over tall mobs (the Minotaur, Kirin, Frost
  Elk, the reapers and fiends, Kings and others) now floats over the top of
  the mob instead of covering its face (v1.456).
- Riding a tall mob puts you on its shoulders or back, instead of inside
  it (v1.456).
- Wild sharks now hunt caught mobs in the water too, not only swimming
  players.
- The Minotaur and the other big boss and mythic fighters built like it
  (Brutaurus, Unixion, Kirin, Frost Elk, Everfrost, Archfiend, Dreadlord,
  Drakari, Dragonlord, Soulreaper, Endbringer, Ender Overlord, Manticore,
  Naga, Ouroboros) could chase an enemy but never land a hit. They now hit
  what they reach, wild or caught.
- Wild ones of these now come after players, instead of only fighting back
  when hit.

## 2026-11-01

### Add-on (v1.427 to v1.453)

**Resting and hunger (v1.453).**

- Out of a fight, you slowly heal on your own: a little of your health every
  3 seconds. It starts once you haven't hit or been hit for 10 seconds and no
  hostile mob is close (about 12 blocks), and it doesn't work near a boss or
  in minigames. Small hearts show while it works.
- Hunger goes down half as fast as before.
- Admins can change both from the Admin Wand (Combat Balancing).

**Riding fixes and a new Grizzly (v1.452).**

- A mob you ride no longer does its own thing under you. Pandas and
  Grizzlies stop rolling, sneezing and lying down, and a hurt mount no longer
  bolts.
- Sea mounts (sharks, dolphins, the Kraken and the like) no longer fly when
  ridden on land. They stay on the ground, move slowly and can hop up one
  block. In water they swim as before. The Sea Horse walks on land like a
  horse.
- The Grizzly has its own model: a big brown bear with a silver-tipped
  hump, glowing eyes and a snarling mouth. It rears up and slams down with
  its paws when it attacks.
- The Minotaur's recipes now use a War Piglin in place of the Piglin Brute:
  War Piglin + Gilded Hoglin, or War Piglin + Siege Ravager, both Lv.20.

**New combat (being tested).** Fights are now worked out from real stats
before a hit lands, and a mob's level is what makes it strong:

- A hit does the attacker's attack minus half the target's defence. If that
  leaves almost nothing, the hit does 0 or 1, so a mob far above yours can't
  be worn down until you've trained up.
- Evade is the chance to dodge a hit or take only half of it. A quick
  attacker with a high evade of its own gets dodged less.
- Stats grow on one smooth curve all the way to Lv.40. The old half-rate
  growth past Lv.20 is gone.
- No more hidden toughness. Bosses no longer heal hits back or shrug off a
  share of each hit. Health only comes back from food, potions, resting and
  traits.
- Admins can switch the new combat on or off and tune it from the Admin Wand
  (Combat Balancing). The settings are global, never per mob.

**Boss levels.** Boss content is now set by level alone:

| Boss | Level |
|---|---|
| Bounty Board posters | 15, 25, 35, 50 |
| Raid Boss | 45 to 65 (15 to 25 above the strongest caught mob there) |
| Ender Raid King | 80 |
| Boss Portal | Legend 70, Mythic 90 |
| Warden (and the mobs that can replace it) | 12 above the strongest caught mob near it, at least 30 |
| Wither | 60 |
| Elder Guardian | 45 |
| Megalodon | 75 |

- Each extra player there when a Raid Boss or Bounty boss appears adds 3
  levels.
- A caught boss comes in at Lv.20 (the Megalodon at Lv.40), and so does a
  caught Mob Tower or Base mob that was higher.
- Bosses use their own traits (Drain, Second Wind and so on), shown under
  their health bar. A boss's Second Wind works once a fight, at half strength.

**Players.**

- Health is a number now: 20, plus 20 for every level. A health bar with the
  number sits where the hearts were.
- Armour matters more: defence grows with your level and the armour you wear
  (leather, iron, diamond, netherite).
- Your attack grows a little with your level and your weapon, but stays below
  a caught mob's. Your mobs do the heavy fighting.

**Screen.**

- A boss health bar of the add-on's own, under the top buttons: the boss's
  name, level, time left and traits. It replaces the game's bar, which showed
  "Unknown" for the add-on's mobs.
- Chat messages are narrower, so they no longer cover the boss bar.

**Mobs and places.**
