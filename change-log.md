# Wildbound change log

What changed on Wildbound: the server's add-on (the mod), the server itself, and
this website. Newest first. Version numbers are the add-on's (v1.451 = 1.451.0).

Each day has up to three parts:

- **Add-on**: changes to the game on the server.
- **Server**: changes to how the server runs or how you join it.
- **Website**: changes to the Mob Index and the welcome pages.

---

## 2026-11-13

### Add-on (v1.539)

- **Better balls in treasure, now random:** tower and base treasure gives 12 Ember Balls, 6 Nether Balls, or no balls, never both.
- **Gear in first person:** set weapons now point the right way in first person, and the scythe's blade faces forward.
- **Staffs shoot:** a short tap with a staff fires a glowing magic bolt that chases the enemy you aim at. Holding still does the special move.
- **New gear effects:** special moves now show solid glowing slashes, shockwave rings, eruption pillars, beams and flying shards in each set's colour.
- **Warp Blocks:** only admins can break them, admins can pair them anywhere (even across dimensions), and travelling makes a magical whoosh.
- **Ladder stacks:** the ladder side starts on the side you're standing on.
- **No health bar on tool blocks:** the Terrain Fill, Demolition and Warp Blocks no longer show a green bar.
- **The End gets bosses:** strong elite and boss mobs now hunt on the End islands (Lv.50 to 60), away from the portals. Most Endermen stay normal.
- **End strongholds:** purpur towers appear on the outer End islands, guarded at Lv.60 with a Lv.65 captain (Lv.75 far out). Beat or catch the captain for treasure. A stronghold is retaken after an hour.
- **Haunted ships:** rare haunted ships can appear far out at sea with a crew to fight and treasure on board.
- Not checked in game yet.

### Add-on (v1.538)

- **Treasure for everyone:** once a tower or base captain is beaten, every player gets the same treasure in its chest, even players who weren't in the fight. Tap the chest, take what you like, and the rest stays saved for you until the tower is retaken and a new captain arrives. One player can look in the chest at a time.
- **Better balls in the treasure:** every Overworld and Nether tower or base treasure now includes 12 Ember Balls and 6 Nether Balls.
- **More better balls:** the Boutique now sells 20 Ember Balls or 20 Nether Balls at the same price (was 5), and crafting makes 16 (was 6). Hard quests that gave 6 Ember or Nether Balls now give 20.
- **Tower cores removed:** the crying obsidian core is gone from towers and bases, and they can no longer be finished for good by hand. Old cores disappear the next time someone visits.
- **13 gear sets:** Wyrmscale, Cinderforge, Soulreaper, Tidefang, Ironhorn, Everfrost, Hexweaver, Voidstep, Gemstalker, Fangwild, Chrome Titan, Sunmane and Foxfire. Each has a weapon, an off-hand, a helmet, a chestplate and leggings, all a step above netherite. Every set plays differently: some hit harder, some take more hits, some dodge more.
- **Hunt Points:** beating elite, boss, legend and mythic mobs gives Hunt Points at the Quest Master (more for higher levels). Everyone close by gets the points. Every 100 points is a Forge Prize.
- **Forging:** the Blacksmith forges a set piece for Forge Prizes, coins, Raid Shards and a diamond piece (or the weapon's base item). Every piece comes with three enchantments, and the Enchantress can add more.
- **Special moves:** hold a set weapon, then let go, for its special move (for example a fire slam, an ice line, a chasing magic flask or a lightning chain). Normal hits can also burn, freeze, poison and more. Sneak with a set off-hand to block most of a hit from the front. Wearing all three armor pieces of one set gives a bonus.
- **Soul Unlock:** set gear grows stronger as you use it. Beat mobs to fill its Soul bar, then the Enchantress unlocks the next Soul level.
- Not checked in game yet.

## 2026-11-01

### Add-on (v1.427 to v1.453)

**Resting and hunger (v1.453).**

- Out of a fight, you slowly heal on your own: a little of your health every
  3 seconds. It starts once you haven't hit or been hit for 10 seconds and no
  hostile mob is close (about 12 blocks), and it doesn't work near a boss or
  in minigames. Small hearts show while it works.
- Hunger goes down half as fast as before.
- Admins can change both from the Admin Wand (Combat Balancing).

**Riding fixes and a new Grizzly (v1.452).**

- A mob you ride no longer does its own thing under you. Pandas and
  Grizzlies stop rolling, sneezing and lying down, and a hurt mount no longer
  bolts.
- Sea mounts (sharks, dolphins, the Kraken and the like) no longer fly when
  ridden on land. They stay on the ground, move slowly and can hop up one
  block. In water they swim as before. The Sea Horse walks on land like a
  horse.
- The Grizzly has its own model: a big brown bear with a silver-tipped
  hump, glowing eyes and a snarling mouth. It rears up and slams down with
  its paws when it attacks.
- The Minotaur's recipes now use a War Piglin in place of the Piglin Brute:
  War Piglin + Gilded Hoglin, or War Piglin + Siege Ravager, both Lv.20.

**New combat (being tested).** Fights are now worked out from real stats
before a hit lands, and a mob's level is what makes it strong:

- A hit does the attacker's attack minus half the target's defence. If that
  leaves almost nothing, the hit does 0 or 1, so a mob far above yours can't
  be worn down until you've trained up.
- Evade is the chance to dodge a hit or take only half of it. A quick
  attacker with a high evade of its own gets dodged less.
- Stats grow on one smooth curve all the way to Lv.40. The old half-rate
  growth past Lv.20 is gone.
- No more hidden toughness. Bosses no longer heal hits back or shrug off a
  share of each hit. Health only comes back from food, potions, resting and
  traits.
- Admins can switch the new combat on or off and tune it from the Admin Wand
  (Combat Balancing). The settings are global, never per mob.

**Boss levels.** Boss content is now set by level alone:

| Boss | Level |
|---|---|
| Bounty Board posters | 15, 25, 35, 50 |
| Raid Boss | 45 to 65 (15 to 25 above the strongest caught mob there) |
| Ender Raid King | 80 |
| Boss Portal | Legend 70, Mythic 90 |
| Warden (and the mobs that can replace it) | 12 above the strongest caught mob near it, at least 30 |
| Wither | 60 |
| Elder Guardian | 45 |
| Megalodon | 75 |

- Each extra player there when a Raid Boss or Bounty boss appears adds 3
  levels.
- A caught boss comes in at Lv.20 (the Megalodon at Lv.40), and so does a
  caught Mob Tower or Base mob that was higher.
- Bosses use their own traits (Drain, Second Wind and so on), shown under
  their health bar. A boss's Second Wind works once a fight, at half strength.

**Players.**

- Health is a number now: 20, plus 20 for every level. A health bar with the
  number sits where the hearts were.
- Armour matters more: defence grows with your level and the armour you wear
  (leather, iron, diamond, netherite).
- Your attack grows a little with your level and your weapon, but stays below
  a caught mob's. Your mobs do the heavy fighting.

**Screen.**

- A boss health bar of the add-on's own, under the top buttons: the boss's
  name, level, time left and traits. It replaces the game's bar, which showed
  "Unknown" for the add-on's mobs.
- Chat messages are narrower, so they no longer cover the boss bar.

**Mobs and places.**

- **Boss Portal** (v1.432): a new town building. Pay the Portal Master and a
  portal opens for 1 hour to an arena with a Legend or Mythic boss of your
  choice. Anyone can go in while it's open.
- **War Piglin** replaces the War Pig: Piglin Brute + Grizzly.
- **Chrome Golem** has a second recipe: Magma Golem + Frost Golem, both Lv.20.
- The Toxic Creeper throws a poison bomb instead of exploding.
- The End Dragon and the Elder Dragon are much bigger, with a seat on the back.
