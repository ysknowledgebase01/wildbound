How fights work: a hit does the attacker's attack minus half the target's defence. If that leaves almost nothing, the hit does 0 or 1, so a mob far stronger than yours can't be worn down. Evade is the chance to dodge a hit or take only half of it, and a quick attacker with a high evade of its own gets dodged less. Stats grow at the same pace all the way to Lv.40. The Lv.20 numbers are for a mob caught plain, or fresh from its mix (+1) if it can only be mixed.

Tap an underlined mob name to jump to its row. Tap a name in the Mob column to search for it: that shows the mob plus every mob it helps make or is made from.

Your wallet

Holds up to 999 coins. With a full wallet the Shop won't buy from you, and coins from requests and bounties past 999 are lost, so spend some first.

Earning coins

Sell finds at the Shop or the Fisherman. Hand in Quest Master requests (6-25 coins). Beat Bounty Board bosses (10-25). Plain wild mobs drop trade goods to sell.

Daily deals

Every day one trade good pays double at the Shop, one Shop item is 20% off, and the Fisherman pays double for one fish.
