Why we built it

Wildbound is a private Minecraft server for a group of friends. We built it for four reasons:

  • For fun. So our kids and their friends have one place to play together, with things to build, catch and play that they can't get anywhere else.
  • To end the fights. Play dates kept starting with "come to my world!" arguments, and ending with broken houses, taken items and hurt feelings. The add-on is designed so that bullying and griefing simply don't work.
  • So nobody misses out. Friends who are out of town, or can't make it to a play date, can still join in from wherever they are.
  • To stay away from public servers. Big public servers are full of strangers and push paid extras. We wanted our kids to have something better to play on, with only friends and nothing to buy.

What it is

It is a Minecraft Bedrock Edition server, the version of Minecraft on tablets, phones and PC (Bedrock). The Java Edition of Minecraft can't join.

It runs the game's official server software on our own private computer, not on a rented game hosting service. On top of that we run our own add-on: a set of friendly changes that make playing together easier for kids, without turning the game into creative mode.

Keeping it kind and safe

  • Only invited players. The server lets in only gamertags we've added, and every player must be signed in to their own account.
  • No player-vs-player damage. Players can't hurt each other anywhere, unless two friends who share a land both switch it on for that land.
  • Spam-hitting gets a time-out. Hitting the same player again and again sends the hitter to the spawn point for 5 minutes.
  • Gentler world. No fall damage, players can't be burned, fire doesn't spread, and TNT can't destroy anything.
  • Land owners have tools. They can choose who may build or open chests, send someone off their land, or ban them from it.
  • An adult checks in most days.
  • Reporting. If your child tells you about unkind behaviour, let us know in the parents' WhatsApp group.
  • Grown-up players help. Parents and family members who play are welcome to keep an eye out for the younger ones.

Chat

Text chat is on, and only invited players can read it. If you'd rather your child didn't use chat, you can limit it in your child's Microsoft account family settings. Those same settings must allow joining multiplayer games, or your child won't be able to connect.

What the server keeps

The server keeps what the game needs: each player's gamertag, when they join and leave, and the world itself, including their buildings and items. We don't ask for names, emails or anything else, and nothing is shared or sold.

How to join

  1. Send us your child's gamertag privately (or yours, if you'd like to play too).
  2. We add it to the list and send you the server address privately.
  3. In Minecraft, open Play, then the Servers tab, then Add Server, and enter the address.
  4. Join. The add-on downloads automatically the first time.

Good to know

  • We try to keep the server on as much as we can, but it is sometimes off for updates and restarts.
  • After a big Minecraft update, your child's game may be newer than the server for a short while and won't connect until we catch up.
  • Game consoles usually can't join outside servers like this one. Tablets, phones and PC (Bedrock) work.
  • Every player needs their own copy of Minecraft Bedrock Edition.

Questions?

Ask in the parents' WhatsApp group. We're happy to explain anything, or to show you around in the game.
