Build, explore, catch, and play games together.

No more "come to my world!" arguments. No more "who broke my house?" Everyone has a home here. It is still real survival, with extra tools that make playing together easier and kinder.

Your own land

Claim a piece of the world

Put down two Claim Markers. Everything in the box between them is yours. Nobody can break your blocks or open your chests unless you say yes.

  1. 1 Place the first marker
  2. 2 Place the second marker
  3. 3 They glow green. It's yours!

For grown-ups: no more broken bases or fights over chest items. Owners choose who can build or open chests, and can send someone off their land.

Never get lost

Walkie Talkie teleport

Use your Walkie Talkie to jump straight to a friend, or back to your own land or a friend's land.

Friends add each other first, so only people you choose can come to you.

For grown-ups: nobody can teleport to a player without their permission.

Build faster

Easy builder blocks

Place two Fill Blocks to fill a whole area with one block in one go. Place two Demolition Blocks to flatten a hill down to the ground.

Your land can also hand out free stacks of building blocks.

For grown-ups: kids get a real base built within their screen time, still in survival mode. The tools never work on someone else's land.

Catch & team up

Catch mobs with a Mob Ball

Throw a Mob Ball to catch a mob. It follows you, fights on your side and levels up.

Mix two caught mobs to make a brand new one. There are over 90 new mobs to find, and a few rare ones show up in the wild if you're lucky.

For grown-ups: a full list of every mob and mixing recipe is on the Mob Index page.

Play together

Minigames on your land

Any land can host a game. Friends walk in during the countdown to join. Nobody gets hurt and nobody loses their stuff.

TagKing's RansomReaper HuntBattle ArenaBow & ArrowMob BattleSpot RushMusical ChairsBalloon Pop

For grown-ups: during a game, chests and building on that land are locked for everyone, so the owner gets no advantage.

Be kind

Bully protection

Players can't hurt each other anywhere on the server. Punching someone over and over sends you to the spawn point for a 5 minute time-out.

No fall damage, no burning, and TNT can't blow anything up.

For grown-ups: two friends who share a land can switch on friendly fire for that land only, if they both want to play-fight.

Safe & private

Invite only, always on

The server runs on our own private computer, not on a rented game server. Only invited players can join, so there are no strangers.

Parents and other family members can play too!

For grown-ups: an adult checks in on the server most days. If something unkind happens, tell us in the parents' WhatsApp group. Parents and family members are welcome to join and help us look out for the younger players. We try to keep the server on as much as we can and keep it updated.
